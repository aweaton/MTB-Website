# Western Newfoundland MTB — Project brief

Working name: MTB-Website (temporary).

## Goals
A visually engaging public guide to mountain biking in Western Newfoundland, combining destination storytelling with practical trail and ride planning.

## Pilot
Pasadena, Newfoundland and Labrador. Expansion to other Western Newfoundland locations is future scope.

## Planned content
- Destination pages with photography and optional embedded videos.
- Interactive map of individual trails and trail networks.
- Featured rides referencing the trail segments they use, including connectors and directional travel.
- Trail descriptions, difficulty, length, elevation, access and verification status when verified.

## Data
Use OSM with its ODbL obligations and authorized personal/contributed GPX tracks. Track sources, permissions, provenance, version/date and verification status. Do not assume OSM-listed routes are safe or legal for biking. Trailforks or other proprietary data needs independent permission before reuse.

## Maintenance
ChatGPT assists with preparing content and GitHub changes. Codex follows local AGENTS.md and the issue-to-PR workflow. Human review and merge gates remain mandatory.

## Deferred decisions
Application framework, map tile provider, hosting, route-data schema and import tooling are to be assessed in separate issues. No site or deployment has yet been implemented.
